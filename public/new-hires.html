First-week checklist

Complete these essentials in your first five working days.

  • Sign in to Gmail, ClickUp, Document Drive and the Time Tracker.
  • Complete your HR profile and emergency-contact information in MOHR.
  • Meet your manager to agree your 30-day priorities and team rhythm.
  • Book your orientation, information-security and workplace-safety sessions.
  • Save this page and explore the Knowledge Hub for your role.

Need help?

Start with the route that best matches your request. If you are unsure where to go, your manager or the People Team can help you find the right owner.

IT Support
For access, equipment or a system issue, use the SolarWinds Service Desk or email the IT Team at ithelpdesk@si-ware.com.
People & HR
For payslips and leave, use MOHR. For other HR requests, use the Company Portal or contact the People Team at human.resources@si-ware.com.
Administration
Use the Company Portal for Administration Team requests.
Finance
Use the Company Portal for Finance Team requests.
