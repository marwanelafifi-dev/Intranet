Before you begin

You do not need to prepare a formal report. It can help to have the relevant details ready:

  • What happened, when and where it happened.
  • The people, teams or systems involved.
  • Relevant documents, messages or other evidence you are permitted to share.
  • Whether you believe there is an ongoing risk or urgent safeguarding need.

Privacy and protection

Use Employee Voice for sensitive details. Do not share a report through personal messaging, a shared team inbox or an unapproved form.

The chatbot provides its privacy guidance as part of the conversation. Read it before you share details.
